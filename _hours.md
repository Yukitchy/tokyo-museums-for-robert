## Ticket sale timing (official)
- Ghibli: tickets go on sale 10:00 JST on the 10th of each month for the following month (e.g. May 2027 entries on 2027-04-10 10:00). Overseas: Lawson Ticket (English) or Sunrise Tours/JTB bus tour. Source: https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/tickets/
- Fujiko F. Fujio: 1st-15th entries go on sale at 10:00 on the 20th of the prior month; 16th-month-end entries at 10:00 on the 5th of the same month. So 5/25-5/31/2027 on sale 2027-05-05 10:00; 6/1-6/5/2027 on sale 2027-05-20 10:00. Source: https://www.e-tix.jp/fujiko-museum/
